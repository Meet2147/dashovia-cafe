Common questions

I paid but don't see my receipt.

Receipts come from Dodo Payments, our payment partner. Check your spam folder for an email from them. Still nothing? Email us with the address you used at checkout.

How do I cancel?

Sign in, open your account menu and choose "Manage subscription", or use the link in your receipt email. You keep access until the end of the period you've paid for.

I can't sign in.

We use Google and GitHub sign-in. Make sure pop-ups aren't blocked for this site, and try a different browser if it keeps failing. If it still doesn't work, email us and tell us which button you used.

How many Macs can I use?

Cutting Chai covers 1 device, Full Kettle up to 5. A one-time Kadak purchase covers 1 device.

Is there a student or team discount?

Email us and tell us about your situation. We're a small café and happy to talk.

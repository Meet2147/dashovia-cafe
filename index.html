What is dashovia.café?

dashovia.café is a subscription for a growing menu of native Mac apps, plus a shelf of free developer tools. Instead of buying apps one by one, a single plan unlocks every premium app on the menu: Cutting Chai is $3.99/month for one device and Full Kettle is $7.99/month for up to five ($1.99 and $3.99 a month paid yearly). Both start with a 7-day free trial.

The menu covers capture and guides (Kadak, Stepwise, Cadence), everyday essentials (Cubby, ResumeBaker) and health (Cairn, coming soon), with more apps added over time. Every app is built to keep your data on your own Mac.

Privacy Policy

Last updated October 18, 2026

Short version: we collect as little as we can. Sign-in gives us your name, email and profile picture. Payments are handled by Dodo Payments, so we never see your card. No ads, no trackers, no selling data.

This policy explains what personal data Dashovia ("we", "us") collects when you use dashovia.café and buy our apps, and what we do with it. Questions? Email contact@dashovia.app.

What we collect

When you sign in

When you sign in with Google or GitHub, we receive your name, email address, profile picture and account ID from that provider. We keep this in a secure, signed cookie on your device so you stay signed in. We don't keep a separate user database or any password.

When you buy something

Checkout is run by Dodo Payments, our Merchant of Record. They collect your payment and billing details directly. We receive your email address, what you bought, the order status and, if you used one, the referral code. We use this to give you access, provide support and handle refunds.

Referrals

If you open a referral link, we remember the referral code in your browser's local storage for 30 days so the discount can be applied at checkout. Your own referral code is derived from your email address; it doesn't reveal it.

Stored in your browser

  • dc_session cookie: keeps you signed in (essential, expires after 30 days or when you sign out).
  • Local storage: your light/dark theme choice and any referral code you arrived with.

We don't use advertising or analytics cookies.

Server logs

Like most websites, our hosting provider records basic technical logs (such as IP address, browser type and the pages requested) to keep the service secure and working. These are kept for a short time.

Our Mac apps

Our Mac apps are designed to keep your data on your device. Each app's website describes exactly what it does with your data.

Who we share it with

We only share data with the services needed to run the Site:

  • Dodo Payments: payments, tax, receipts and subscription management.
  • Google and GitHub: only if you choose to sign in with them.
  • Render: hosts the Site.
  • Google Fonts: serves the fonts on the Site (your browser requests them from Google).

We never sell your personal data or share it for advertising.

How long we keep it

Sign-in data lives in your browser cookie until it expires or you sign out. Order records are kept by Dodo Payments and by us for as long as needed for your purchase, support, and tax and accounting obligations.

Your rights

You can ask us to access, correct, export or delete your personal data, or object to how we use it. Email contact@dashovia.app from the address on your account and we'll respond within 30 days. Depending on where you live (for example the EU, UK, California or India), you may also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority.

Children

The Site isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date above and, for significant changes, let subscribers know by email.

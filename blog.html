Welcome to Dashovia Café ☕

We make small, focused apps: the kind that do one job and do it well. Over time we ended up with a shelf of them: Mac apps for capturing websites, writing guides and keeping your desk healthy, and a handful of Python tools we use every day.

Buying each one separately felt like a chore for you and for us. So we opened a café instead.

How it works

  • Cutting Chai: every premium app on 1 device, $3.99/month or $1.99/month billed yearly.
  • Full Kettle: the same, on up to 5 devices, $7.99/month or $3.99/month billed yearly.
  • On the house: the developer tools stay free.

Every plan starts with a 7-day free trial, and every payment is covered by a 14-day money-back guarantee. When we ship a new app, subscribers get it automatically.

Why our Mac apps stay on your Mac 🔒

Look at the apps on our menu and you'll notice a pattern:

  • Cubby keeps your most-copied values (phone numbers, API keys, bank details) in a menu-bar panel, 100% on-device.
  • Cadence records 5-step screen guides and stitches them into one MP4. No editing, no cloud.
  • Cairn coaches your posture, eyes and stretch breaks using on-device AI. Your webcam feed never leaves your Mac.
  • Stepwise turns your clicks into a captioned, screenshotted guide and a PDF, privately, on-device.

That's on purpose. The things these apps touch, like your screen, your webcam and your secrets, are personal. Keeping them on your device means there's no account to create, no server to breach, and no waiting on an upload. It also means the apps keep working on a plane.

It's a little harder for us to build this way. We think it's worth it.

Meet Kadak: any website, one tidy PDF 💪

"Kadak" is what we call chai that's brewed strong. Kadak the app is strong at one thing: turning any website into a clean, full-page PDF.

  1. Paste a URL.
  2. Choose how many screenshots you want (up to 10).
  3. Brew. You get a full-page PDF plus the screenshots, saved to your Downloads folder.

It's handy for archiving receipts and articles, sharing a design review, or keeping a record of how a page looked on a given day.

Kadak is included with every café subscription, or you can buy it on its own for a one-time $4.99.

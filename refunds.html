Refund Policy

Last updated October 18, 2026

Not happy? Get your money back within 14 days.

Every payment, subscription or one-time purchase, is covered by a 14-day money-back guarantee. No questions asked.

Free trial first

Subscriptions start with a 7-day free trial. Cancel before it ends and you won't be charged at all.

Subscriptions

  • Ask within 14 days of any charge (including renewals) for a full refund of that charge.
  • After 14 days, payments aren't refundable, but you can cancel any time to stop future renewals. You keep access until the end of the period you've paid for.

One-time purchases (like Kadak)

Ask within 14 days of purchase for a full refund. After a refund, the licence is deactivated.

Request a refund

Sign in with the account you paid with to see your payments.

How to ask for a refund

  1. Sign in and use the form above, or email contact@dashovia.app from the email address you used at checkout.
  2. Include your order or receipt number (it's in your receipt email from Dodo Payments).
  3. That's it. You don't need to give a reason.

We aim to reply within 2 business days. Refunds are issued by our payment partner Dodo Payments to your original payment method and usually appear within 5–10 business days, depending on your bank.

Exceptions

We may decline refunds in cases of clear abuse, such as repeated buy-and-refund cycles. If a refund is for a purchase made through a referral link, the referral reward for that purchase is cancelled.

Questions

Email contact@dashovia.app. We're happy to help.
